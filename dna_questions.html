Answers (Questions 1–6)

1 Rank (smallest → biggest)

Nucleotide → Gene → DNA → Chromosome → Nucleus → Cell

2 Where does DNA replication take place?

In the nucleus.

3 When does DNA replication take place?

During S phase of interphase (before cell division).

4 Complementary base sequence

Given: G-A-C-T-T-A-C-G-T-A-C-C

Complement: C-T-G-A-A-T-G-C-A-T-G-G

Base-pair rules: A↔T, C↔G.

5 Result of DNA replication

Two identical DNA molecules. Each new DNA has one original (parent) strand and one new strand (semi-conservative replication).

6 Book analogy

a) Chapters = Chromosomes

b) Sentences = Genes

c) Letters = Bases (A, T, C, G)

Answers (Questions 7–8)

7a Name of Molecule X

DNA (Deoxyribonucleic acid)

7b Name of Molecule Y

RNA (messenger RNA / mRNA)

7c Name of Molecule Z

Protein

7d Function of Molecule X

Stores genetic information (instructions) that control traits, growth, and development.

7e Function of Molecule Y

Carries instructions from DNA to the ribosome where proteins are made.

7f Function of Molecule Z

Proteins build structures and control reactions (enzymes/hormones), producing traits and functions.

8 How are genes involved in producing proteins?

Genes are sections of DNA that contain instructions for making specific proteins. A gene is transcribed into RNA, and the RNA is translated into a protein.
