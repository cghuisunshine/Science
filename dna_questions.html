1 Rank (smallest → biggest)从小到大排序

Nucleotide → Gene → DNA → Chromosome → Nucleus → Cell

核苷酸 → 基因 → DNA → 染色体 → 细胞核 → 细胞

Explanation: Nucleotides are the building blocks; genes are sections of DNA; DNA coils into chromosomes; chromosomes are in the nucleus; the nucleus is inside the cell.

解释：核苷酸是基本构件；基因是 DNA 的片段；DNA 盘绕形成染色体；染色体位于细胞核内；细胞核在细胞内部。

2 Where does DNA replication take place?DNA 复制发生在哪里？

In the nucleus.

在细胞核中。

Explanation: In eukaryotic cells, DNA is stored in the nucleus, so it is copied there before the cell divides.

解释：在真核细胞中，DNA 储存在细胞核内，因此细胞分裂前会在细胞核中完成复制。

3 When does DNA replication take place?DNA 复制在什么时候发生？

During S phase of interphase (before cell division).

在间期的 S 期（细胞分裂前）。

Explanation: The S (“synthesis”) phase is when the cell duplicates its DNA so each daughter cell can receive a complete set.

解释：S（“合成”）期是细胞复制 DNA 的阶段，以便每个子细胞都能获得完整的一套遗传信息。

4 Complementary base sequence互补碱基序列

Given: G-A-C-T-T-A-C-G-T-A-C-C

Complement: C-T-G-A-A-T-G-C-A-T-G-G

已知：G-A-C-T-T-A-C-G-T-A-C-C

互补链：C-T-G-A-A-T-G-C-A-T-G-G

Explanation: Use base-pair rules: A↔T, C↔G.

解释：使用碱基互补配对规则：A↔T，C↔G。

5 Result of DNA replicationDNA 复制的结果

Two identical DNA molecules. Each new DNA has one original (parent) strand and one new strand (semi-conservative replication).

产生两条完全相同的 DNA 分子。每条新 DNA 都由一条原来的（亲代）链和一条新合成的链组成（半保留复制）。

Explanation: Keeping one original strand helps the new strand be copied accurately using complementary base pairing.

解释：保留一条原有链作为模板，利用互补配对规则可帮助新链更准确地复制。

6 Book analogy书本类比

a) Chapters = Chromosomes

b) Sentences = Genes

c) Letters = Bases (A, T, C, G)

a) 章节 = 染色体

b) 句子 = 基因

c) 字母 = 碱基（A、T、C、G）

Explanation: Large structures are made of smaller parts—letters make sentences, sentences make chapters; bases make genes, and genes are organized on chromosomes.

解释：大的结构由小的部分组成——字母组成句子，句子组成章节；碱基组成基因，基因组织在染色体上。

Answers (Questions 7–8)

7a Name of Molecule X分子 X 的名称

DNA (Deoxyribonucleic acid)

DNA（脱氧核糖核酸）

Explanation: DNA is the long-term information storage molecule in cells.

解释：DNA 是细胞中长期储存遗传信息的分子。

7b Name of Molecule Y分子 Y 的名称

RNA (messenger RNA / mRNA)

RNA（信使 RNA / mRNA）

Explanation: mRNA is a temporary copy of a gene that carries the message to where proteins are made.

解释：mRNA 是基因的临时拷贝，把信息传递到合成蛋白质的地方。

7c Name of Molecule Z分子 Z 的名称

Protein

蛋白质

Explanation: Proteins are the final products of gene instructions and do most cellular jobs.

解释：蛋白质是基因指令的最终产物，承担细胞中大多数功能。

7d Function of Molecule X分子 X 的功能

Stores genetic information (instructions) that control traits, growth, and development.

储存遗传信息（指令），控制性状、生长与发育。

Explanation: The order of DNA bases is a code that can be read to build specific proteins.

解释：DNA 碱基的排列顺序是一种“编码”，可被读取用来合成特定蛋白质。

7e Function of Molecule Y分子 Y 的功能

Carries instructions from DNA to the ribosome where proteins are made.

把 DNA 的指令携带到核糖体，在那里合成蛋白质。

Explanation: Ribosomes read mRNA codons to assemble amino acids in the correct order.

解释：核糖体读取 mRNA 的密码子，按正确顺序连接氨基酸。

7f Function of Molecule Z分子 Z 的功能

Proteins build structures and control reactions (enzymes/hormones), producing traits and functions.

蛋白质构建结构并控制反应（如酶/激素），从而产生各种性状与功能。

Explanation: A protein’s shape determines its function, such as catalyzing reactions (enzymes) or sending signals (some hormones).

解释：蛋白质的形状决定其功能，例如催化反应（酶）或传递信号（某些激素）。

8 How are genes involved in producing proteins?基因如何参与蛋白质的产生？

Genes are sections of DNA that contain instructions for making specific proteins. A gene is transcribed into RNA, and the RNA is translated into a protein.

基因是 DNA 的片段，包含制造特定蛋白质的指令。基因先被转录成 RNA，再由 RNA 翻译成蛋白质。

Explanation: The gene’s base sequence determines the mRNA sequence, which determines the amino acid order and therefore the protein made.

解释：基因的碱基序列决定 mRNA 序列，mRNA 序列又决定氨基酸排列顺序，从而决定合成的蛋白质。
