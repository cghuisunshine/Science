RNA stands for…（RNA 的全称是……）

Vocabulary
—
Solution: RNA stands for ribonucleic acid. RNA is made of nucleotides (ribose sugar + phosphate + nitrogen base) and is found in most living organisms and many viruses.
解析：RNA 的全称是 ribonucleic acid（核糖核酸）。RNA 由核苷酸组成（核糖 + 磷酸 + 含氮碱基），存在于大多数生物以及许多病毒中。

Cell nucleus
—
Solution: All of these. The nucleus contains DNA packaged as chromatin, and RNA is made there during transcription (and some RNA is present/processed in the nucleus).
解析：以上皆是。细胞核含有以染色质形式包装的 DNA；RNA 在转录过程中在细胞核内产生（一些 RNA 也在细胞核内存在/加工）。

How do plant cells differ from animal cells?（植物细胞与动物细胞有什么不同？）

Cells
—
Solution: Animal cells do not have chloroplasts. Plant cells have chloroplasts for photosynthesis, plus a cell wall and a large central vacuole.
解析：动物细胞没有叶绿体。植物细胞有叶绿体用于光合作用，还具有细胞壁和较大的中央液泡。

True or False: Cell walls provide support for animal cells.（判断：细胞壁为动物细胞提供支撑。对/错？）

Cell structures
—
Solution: False. Animal cells do not have cell walls. Cell walls are rigid structures in plant cells that provide structure, shape, support, and protection.
解析：错。动物细胞没有细胞壁。植物细胞的细胞壁是坚硬结构，提供结构、形状、支撑和保护。

________ are sections of DNA that code for specific traits.（________ 是 DNA 的片段，编码特定性状。）

DNA → traits
—
Solution: The answer is genes. Genes are segments of DNA that contain instructions for making proteins, and proteins help produce traits.
解析：答案是 genes（基因）。基因是 DNA 的片段，包含制造蛋白质的指令；蛋白质帮助形成性状。

If two people who are both carriers for a genetically inherited fatal recessive disease decide to become parents, what are the odds that their children will also be carriers?（若父母双方都是致死性隐性遗传病的携带者，孩子也成为携带者的概率是多少？）

Recessive

Punnett Square (parents are carriers: Aa × Aa)（潘尼特方格：父母为携带者） A = normal allele, a = recessive disease allele（A = 正常等位基因，a = 隐性致病等位基因）

Aa
AAAAa
aAaaa
Outcomes: 1 AA (not a carrier) • 2 Aa (carriers) • 1 aa (affected).
Carrier chance = 2/4 = 50%.
结果：1 个 AA（非携带者）• 2 个 Aa（携带者）• 1 个 aa（患病）。
携带者概率 = 2/4 = 50%。
—
Solution: If both parents are carriers (Aa), offspring genotypes are AA, Aa, Aa, aa. Carriers are Aa → 2 out of 4 children (50%) are carriers.
解析：父母都是携带者（Aa）时，子代基因型为 AA、Aa、Aa、aa。携带者是 Aa，因此 2/4（50%）的孩子是携带者。

One flower is heterozygous red (Rr) and it is crossed with a homozygous white (rr) plant. What is the probability an offspring is red?（杂合红色 Rr 与纯合白色 rr 杂交，后代为红色的概率是多少？）

Punnett

Punnett Square (Rr × rr)（潘尼特方格） R = red (dominant), r = white (recessive)（R = 红色/显性，r = 白色/隐性）

rr
RRrRr
rrrrr
Red phenotypes: Rr and Rr → 2 out of 4 = 50% red.
红色表现型：Rr 和 Rr → 2/4 = 50% 为红色。
—
Solution: Cross Rr × rr. Offspring are 50% Rr (red) and 50% rr (white). So probability of red = 50%.
解析：Rr × rr 的后代为 50% Rr（红） 和 50% rr（白），所以红色概率 = 50%。

If two parents are heterozygous for a genetically inherited dominant trait, what is the probability their child shows the trait in the phenotype?（父母对某显性性状均为杂合时，孩子在表现型上表现该性状的概率是多少？）

Dominant

Punnett Square (Aa × Aa)（潘尼特方格） A = dominant allele, a = recessive allele（A = 显性等位基因，a = 隐性等位基因）

Aa
AAAAa
aAaaa
Dominant phenotype appears with AA or Aa → 3/4 = 75%.
显性表现型：AA 或 Aa → 3/4 = 75%。
—
Solution: Offspring genotypes: AA, Aa, Aa, aa. Any genotype with at least one dominant allele (A) shows the trait → 3 out of 4 = 75%.
解析：子代基因型为 AA、Aa、Aa、aa。只要至少有一个显性等位基因 A（AA 或 Aa）就会表现该性状，因此 3/4 = 75%。

True or False: DNA is tightly packaged into structures called genes.（判断：DNA 被紧密包装成称为“基因”的结构。对/错？）

DNA structure
—
Solution: False. DNA is packaged into thread-like structures called chromosomes. Chromosomes are DNA tightly coiled around proteins called histones. Genes are segments of DNA, not the packaging structure.
解析：错。DNA 被包装成称为染色体的线状结构。染色体是 DNA 缠绕在称为组蛋白的蛋白质上形成的。基因是DNA 的片段，而不是包装结构。

As a base pair, thymine always pairs up with…（胸腺嘧啶 T 总是与哪种碱基配对？）

Base pairing
—
Solution: A pairs with T (adenine with thymine) using two hydrogen bonds. Also, G pairs with C (guanine with cytosine) using three hydrogen bonds.
解析：A 与 T 配对（腺嘌呤与胸腺嘧啶），通过两条氢键；G 与 C 配对（鸟嘌呤与胞嘧啶），通过三条氢键。
