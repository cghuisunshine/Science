Inquiry Question 探究问题
Given that you cannot get something from nothing, how do chemical reactions work? 既然不可能从“无”中得到“有”，化学反应是怎样发生的？
Conservation of Mass Claim 质量守恒结论

Chemical reactions work because atoms are rearranged, not created or destroyed. Reactants supply all of the atoms that appear in the products. When the exact ratio is used, all atoms are used in the products. When too much of one reactant is added, leftover reactants do not disappear; they remain after the reaction. 化学反应之所以能够发生，是因为原子被重新排列，而不是被创造或消灭。反应物提供了产物中出现的全部原子。比例刚好时，所有原子都会进入产物；某种反应物过量时，剩余的反应物不会消失，而会在反应后保留下来。

The PhET simulation showed the same pattern in sandwiches, molecules, and game levels: balanced recipes conserve the number of each part or atom. PhET 模拟在三明治、分子和游戏关卡中都显示了同样的规律：平衡的“配方”会守恒每一种部件或原子的数量。
