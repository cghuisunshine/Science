2.5 Types of Chemical Reactions2.5 化学反应的类型

Synthesis化合反应

Synthesis reactions are also known as formation reactions.Synthesis reactions 也叫 formation reactions。

Definition: A synthesis reaction is when two or more reactants (usually elements) join to form a compound.定义：化合反应是指两个或更多反应物（通常是元素）结合形成一种化合物。

  • A + B → AB, where A and B represent elements

The elements may form ionic compounds, like these:这些元素可以形成离子化合物，例如：

  • Sodium metal and chlorine gas combine to form sodium chloride.
    2 Na(s) + Cl2(g) → 2 NaCl(s)
  • Magnesium metal reacts with oxygen gas to form magnesium oxide.
    2 Mg(s) + O2(g) → 2 MgO(s)

Or the elements may form covalent compounds, like this:或者这些元素也可以形成共价化合物，例如：

  • Nitrogen gas and oxygen gas join to form dinitrogen monoxide.
    2 N2(s) + O2(g) → 2 N2O(g)

Decomposition分解反应

Decomposition reactions are the opposite of synthesis reactions.分解反应与化合反应相反。

Definition: A decomposition reaction is when compounds break down into two or more products (often elements).定义：分解反应是指化合物分解成两种或更多生成物（通常是元素）。

  • AB → A + B, where A and B represent elements

Ionic compounds may decompose to produce elements, like this:离子化合物可以分解生成元素，例如：

  • Table salt, sodium chloride, can be broken down into sodium metal and chlorine gas by melting salt at 800ºC and running electricity through it.
    2 NaCl(s) → 2Na(s) + Cl2(g)

Covalent compounds may decompose into elements, like this:共价化合物也可以分解成元素，例如：

  • By running electricity through water, the water molecules decompose into hydrogen and oxygen gases.
    2 H2O(l) → 2 H2(g) + O2(g)

Single Replacement单取代反应

Single replacement reactions replace one element from a compound with a separate element, added as a reactant.单取代反应会让一个单独加入的元素取代化合物中的某个元素。

Definition: A single replacement reaction is when a compound and an element react, and the element switches places with part of the original compound. Positive ions will only replace a positive ion and negative ions will only replace a negative ion.定义：单取代反应是指一种化合物和一种元素发生反应，这个元素与原化合物中的一部分交换位置。正离子只会取代正离子，负离子只会取代负离子。

  • A + BC → B + AC where A is a metal
  • A + BC → C + BA where A is a non-metal

When A is a metal:当 A 是金属时：

  • Aluminum foil in a solution of copper (II) chloride produces solid copper and aluminum chloride.
    2 Al(s) + 3 CuCl2(aq) → 3 Cu(s) + 2 AlCl3(aq)

When A is a non-metal:当 A 是非金属时：

  • When fluorine is bubbled through a sodium iodide solution, iodine and sodium fluoride are produced.
    F2(g) + 2 NaI(aq) → I2(s) + 2 NaF(aq)

Double Replacement双取代反应

Double replacement reactions swap elements between two compounds reacting together to form two new compounds.双取代反应是两个化合物互相交换组成部分，从而形成两个新化合物。

Definition: Double Replacement is when two compounds react, with elements switching places between the original compounds.定义：双取代反应是指两个化合物反应时，原来化合物中的元素互相交换位置。

  • Two elements switch partner
  • AB + CD → AD + CB
  • When potassium chromate and silver nitrate react they switch partners to form silver chromate and potassium nitrate.
    K2CrO4(aq) + 2 AgNO3(aq) → Ag2CrO4(s) + 2 KNO3(aq)

Neutralization中和反应

Neutralization reactions occur when an acid and a base react to form a salt and water.中和反应发生在酸和碱反应生成盐和水时。

Definition: Neutralization is when an acid (most compounds starting with H) and a base (most compounds ending in OH) react.定义：中和反应是酸（多数以 H 开头的化合物）与碱（多数以 OH 结尾的化合物）发生反应。

  • acid + base → salt + water
  • HX + MOH → MX + H2O where X and M are elements or polyatomic ions
  • Sulphuric acid is used to neutralize calcium hydroxide:
    H2SO4(aq) + Ca(OH)2(aq) → CaSO4(aq) + 2 H2O(l)
  • Phosphoric acid helps to neutralize the compounds that cause rust, such as iron (II) hydroxide.
    H3PO4(aq) + 3 Fe(OH)2(aq) → Fe3(PO4)2(s) + 6H2O(l)

Combustion燃烧反应

Combustion reactions occur when a compound or element reacts with oxygen to release energy and produce an oxide.燃烧反应发生在一种化合物或元素与氧气反应，释放能量并生成氧化物时。

This reaction is also sometimes referred to as hydrocarbon combustion.这种反应有时也叫做 hydrocarbon combustion。

Definition: A combustion reaction is when a hydrocarbon reacts with oxygen to produce water and carbon dioxide定义：燃烧反应是指一种烃和氧气反应，生成水和二氧化碳。

CxHy + O2 → CO2 + H2O

where X and Y represent integers其中 X 和 Y 代表整数。

Examples例子

  • Natural gas (methane) is burned in furnaces to heat homes.
CH4 + 2 O2 → CO2 + 2 H2O
  • An acetylene torch is used to weld metals together
2 C2H2 + 5 O2 → 4CO2 + 2 H2O
  • Carbohydrates like glucose combine with oxygen in our body to release energy.
C6H12O6 + 6 O2 → 6 CO2 + 6 H2O

Mole Ratios摩尔比

The numbers of moles of substances involved in a reaction, when compared and reduced to lowest terms, yield a mole ratio.反应中各种物质的摩尔数相互比较并化为最简后，就得到摩尔比。

For example, given the equation, 4H2 + 2O2 ---> 4H20 the mole ratio of H2 to O2 to H2O is 4 to 2 to 4, which reduces to 2 to 1 to 2 or 2:1:2.

Given the following equations, indicate the mole ratios involved.

1. H2 + S ----> H2

1:1:1

2. 4Al + 3O2 ---->2AL2O3

4:3:2

3. 3Zn + 6HCl -----> 3ZnCl2 + 3H2

1:2:1:1

4. SO+1/2O2 ----->S0

2:1:2

5. 6H2 + 3C -----> 3CH4

2:1:1
