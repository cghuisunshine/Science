Camera Capture

Gamepad: not connected

Take many photos, shift-click or cmd/ctrl-click to multi-select, drag a selected stack together, use the blue corner to resize, the orange handle to rotate, or scroll/pinch on the photo to zoom. Arrow keys move the selected set, cmd/ctrl+Z undoes, shift+cmd/ctrl+Z redoes. Gamepad: A / RB / RT = photo, X = next photo, B = clear selection, left stick / D-pad = move, right stick left/right = rotate.
