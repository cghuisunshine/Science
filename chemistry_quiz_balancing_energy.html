2. Which equations are balanced? 2. 哪些方程式已经配平？
I   P4O10 + 10 C → P4 + 10 CO
II   C2H2 + 5 O → 2 CO2 + 2 H2O
III   Ca3(PO4)2 + 4 H3PO4 → 3 Ca(H2PO4)2
Explanation:解释： I is balanced and III is balanced. II is not balanced because hydrogen and oxygen do not match. In II, the left side has H = 2 and O = 5, but the right side has H = 4 and O = 6. I 已经配平，III 也已经配平。II 没有配平，因为氢和氧的数量不相等。在 II 中，左边 H = 2、O = 5，但右边 H = 4、O = 6。
3. Which one of the following statements is true about an exothermic reaction? 3. 关于放热反应，下列哪一项说法是正确的？
Explanation:解释： An exothermic reaction releases heat energy. Because heat is released, the temperature usually increases. 放热反应会释放热能。因为热被释放出来，温度通常会上升。
4. Which of the following represents an endothermic reaction? 4. 下列哪一个表示吸热反应？
Explanation:解释： Endothermic reactions absorb heat. A positive ΔH means heat is absorbed. Since ΔH = +177.7 kJ, this reaction is endothermic. 吸热反应会吸收热量。正的 ΔH 表示热量被吸收。因为 ΔH = +177.7 kJ，所以这个反应是吸热反应。
