1
Use a flowchart with diagrams to summarize gene cloning.用流程图（配合示意图）总结基因克隆。
  1. Select the target gene (e.g., insulin gene).
  2. Use a restriction enzyme to cut donor DNA and isolate the gene.
  3. Use the same restriction enzyme to cut a bacterial plasmid.
  4. Use DNA ligase to join the gene into the plasmid → recombinant plasmid.
  5. Put the recombinant plasmid into bacteria (transformation).
  6. Grow the bacteria; as they reproduce, they copy the plasmid (clone the gene).
  7. Collect the cloned DNA or the protein made from the gene.
  1. 选择目标基因（例如胰岛素基因）。
  2. 用限制性内切酶切割供体 DNA 并分离出该基因。
  3. 用同一种限制性内切酶切开细菌质粒。
  4. 用DNA 连接酶把基因连接进质粒 → 形成重组质粒。
  5. 将重组质粒导入细菌（转化）。
  6. 培养细菌；细菌繁殖时会复制质粒（实现基因克隆）。
  7. 收集克隆得到的 DNA 或该基因表达产生的蛋白质。
2
Benefit of producing insulin from transgenic plants rather than transgenic bacteria.用转基因植物而不是转基因细菌生产胰岛素的好处。
  • Plants can be cheaper/easier to scale up (grown like crops).
  • Lower risk of some bacterial toxin/endotoxin contamination.
  • Storage/transport can be simpler depending on the plant system.
  • 植物可像农作物一样种植，成本更低、扩大规模更容易。
  • 部分细菌毒素/内毒素污染风险更低。
  • 视植物系统而定，储存/运输可能更方便。
3
Transgenic carrot that secretes toxins that kill damaging insects and worms.转基因胡萝卜分泌毒素以杀死有害昆虫和线虫。
  • Benefits: less pesticide spraying; less crop loss; higher yields; potentially less chemical runoff.
  • Risks/concerns: harm to non‑target organisms; pests evolve resistance; gene flow to wild relatives; ecosystem impacts; food safety/allergen checks; monitoring + public acceptance.
  • 好处：减少喷洒农药；减少作物损失；提高产量；可能减少化学物质径流。
  • 风险/顾虑：可能伤害非目标生物；害虫可能演化出抗性；基因流向野生近缘种；生态系统影响；食品安全/致敏性检测；持续监测与公众接受度。
  • Advantages: fewer pests → better yield/quality; reduced pesticide cost and labor.
  • Drawbacks: higher seed cost; dependence on seed companies; some buyers avoid GM products; resistance may develop over time.
  • 优点：害虫更少 → 产量/品质更好；减少农药成本与喷洒劳力。
  • 缺点：种子成本更高；对种子公司依赖增加；部分消费者/买家回避转基因产品；长期可能出现抗性。
  • For labelling: right‑to‑know; informed choice; transparency and trust; helps ethical/diet preferences.
  • Against labelling: may imply risk; adds cost; can confuse consumers.
  • 支持标注：知情权；便于做出知情选择；提高透明度与信任；帮助满足伦理/饮食偏好。
  • 反对标注：可能暗示存在风险；增加成本；可能让消费者困惑。

Explain the steps involved in in vitro fertilization (IVF).解释体外受精（IVF）的步骤。
  1. Hormones stimulate ovaries to produce multiple eggs.
  2. Eggs are collected from the ovaries.
  3. Sperm is collected and prepared.
  4. Egg and sperm are combined in the lab (or sperm injected into egg).
  5. Embryos develop for several days.
  6. One or more embryos are transferred into the uterus.
  7. If implantation occurs, pregnancy begins; extra embryos may be frozen.
  1. 用激素刺激卵巢产生多个卵子。
  2. 从卵巢取出卵子。
  3. 采集并处理精子样本。
  4. 在实验室将卵子与精子结合（或将精子注入卵子）。
  5. 胚胎发育数天。
  6. 将一个或多个胚胎移植到子宫内。
  7. 若成功着床则开始妊娠；多余胚胎可冷冻保存。

Explain how gene therapy works (graphic organizer).解释基因治疗如何起作用（图示整理）。
  • Problem: faulty gene → wrong/missing protein → disease.
  • Solution: deliver a working gene (or edit the gene) into target cells.
  • Carrier: often a modified virus (vector) that can enter cells.
  • Outcome: cells make the correct protein → symptoms improve.
  • Concerns: delivery to enough cells, immune reactions, long‑term safety.
  • 问题：基因缺陷 → 蛋白质错误/缺失 → 疾病。
  • 解决：把正常基因（或进行基因编辑）送入目标细胞。
  • 载体：常用改造过的病毒（载体）帮助进入细胞。
  • 结果：细胞产生正确蛋白 → 症状改善。
  • 顾虑：能否递送到足够多细胞、免疫反应、长期安全性。

7
How can transgenic organisms help achieve goals? Give one example.转基因生物如何帮助实现目标？举一个例子。

Transgenic organisms can support social goals (better health/nutrition), economic goals (higher yield/lower losses), and environmental goals (reduced pesticide use).

Example: Golden rice is engineered to produce beta‑carotene (vitamin A precursor) to reduce vitamin A deficiency.

转基因生物可以支持社会目标（改善健康/营养）、经济目标（更高产量/更少损失）以及环境目标（减少农药使用）。

例子：黄金大米被改造以产生 β‑胡萝卜素（维生素 A 的前体），以减少维生素 A 缺乏。

Transgenic peanuts that do not produce allergic reactions: good use of research money?不引起过敏反应的转基因花生：是否值得投入研究经费？

Yes, likely. Severe food allergies can be life‑threatening. If transgenic peanuts reduce allergic reactions, it could prevent emergencies and improve quality of life. It still needs strong safety testing and clear labelling/communication.

很可能是值得的。 严重食物过敏可能危及生命。若转基因花生能降低过敏反应，就可能减少急症并提高生活质量。但仍需要严格的安全测试以及清晰的标签与沟通。

The diagram shows gene therapy for an inherited retinal disease. A mutation causes light‑sensing retinal cells to die. Doctors inject fluid to lift the affected layer and then inject normal copies of the gene (DNA) to help cells make the correct protein and survive, slowing or stopping vision loss.

图示展示的是针对遗传性视网膜疾病的基因治疗。突变会导致感光视网膜细胞死亡。医生先注入液体抬起受影响的组织层，然后注入正常的基因拷贝（DNA），帮助细胞制造正确的蛋白并存活，从而减缓或阻止视力丧失。
