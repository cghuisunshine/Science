Essential Question 核心问题

How is DNA the foundation for unity and diversity of living things? 为什么说 DNA 是生命统一性与多样性的基础？

DNA is the universal molecule that stores hereditary information in almost all living things. Because all organisms use DNA, we see unity in cellular processes; because DNA sequences differ, we see diversity in traits, species, and ecosystems.

DNA 是几乎所有生物用来储存遗传信息的通用分子。统一性 来自“大家都用 DNA”， 多样性 则来自“每个物种、每个个体的 DNA 序列不同”，因此形态、性状和生态位也不同。

DNA structure → chromosomes → replication → genetic variation → biodiversity.

Key Ideas 关键概念
  • DNA is a long molecule made of many nucleotides arranged in a specific order.
  • The double helix with complementary base pairing (A–T, C–G) allows DNA to be copied accurately.
  • DNA wraps around proteins to form chromatin and condenses into chromosomes.
  • Each chromosome carries many genes; different alleles lead to variation within a species.
  • Faithful DNA replication passes genetic information from cell to cell and from generation to generation.

关键概念：DNA 是由许多核苷酸按特定顺序连接而成的长链分子；双螺旋+碱基互补配对 （A–T，C–G）保证复制的准确；DNA 缠绕蛋白形成染色质，并在分裂时压缩成染色体； 每条染色体上有许多基因，同一基因的不同等位基因导致种内差异； 精确的 DNA 复制让遗传信息在细胞和世代之间传递。

Key Terms 重要术语
DNA nucleotide nitrogenous base complementary bases chromatin chromosome gene allele homologous chromosome karyotype species population

重要术语：核苷酸、含氮碱基、互补碱基、染色质、染色体、基因、等位基因、 同源染色体、核型、物种、种群等。

From DNA to Chromosomes

In human cells, about 2 metres of DNA are packed into a nucleus only a few micrometres across. DNA coils around proteins, forming chromatin fibres that condense into visible chromosomes during cell division.

人类每个细胞有大约 2 米长的 DNA，却被装进直径只有几微米的细胞核中。 DNA 缠绕在蛋白质上形成染色质纤维，在细胞分裂时进一步缠绕压缩成可见的染色体。

DNA Replication and Protein Construction

Before a cell divides, DNA replicates so each daughter cell receives a complete genome. Later, segments of DNA (genes) are transcribed and translated into proteins, which build cell structures and carry out functions.

细胞分裂前必须复制 DNA，让两个子细胞都得到一套完整基因组。 之后，基因先被转录成 RNA，再被翻译成蛋白质；蛋白质构成细胞结构，也执行大部分功能。

How is hereditary information passed from one generation to the next? 遗传信息是如何在世代之间传递的？

Key Ideas 关键概念
  • Genes are segments of DNA that code for traits; different versions are called alleles.
  • During meiosis, homologous chromosomes (and their alleles) separate, so each gamete gets one allele per gene.
  • Punnett squares model how alleles combine at fertilization and predict offspring ratios.
  • Dominance patterns include complete dominance, incomplete dominance, codominance, and sex-linked inheritance.

关键概念：基因是决定性状的 DNA 片段，不同版本叫等位基因； 在减数分裂时，同源染色体分离，等位基因随之分开，每个配子只带其中一个； 用潘尼特（Punnett）方格可模拟受精时等位基因如何组合，并预测子代表型比例； 显性–隐性、不完全显性、共显性、伴性遗传等不同遗传方式，会产生不同家族谱型。

Key Terms 重要术语
genetics trait dominant recessive phenotype genotype homozygous heterozygous codominance incomplete dominance sex-linked trait

重要术语：遗传学、性状、显性、隐性、表现型、基因型、纯合、杂合、 共显性、不完全显性、伴性性状等。

Mendel’s Legacy

Gregor Mendel’s pea plant experiments showed that traits are inherited as discrete units (genes) and that some alleles mask others (dominant vs recessive). His classic 3:1 ratio in the F2 generation is the foundation of Mendelian genetics.

孟德尔用豌豆做实验，提出“性状由离散单位（基因）控制”，且某些等位基因可掩盖另一个 （显性/隐性）。F₂ 代常见的 3:1 表型比例就是经典孟德尔遗传的体现。

Beyond Simple Dominance

  • Incomplete dominance: heterozygotes show an intermediate phenotype (e.g., red × white → pink flowers).
  • Codominance: both alleles are fully expressed in the heterozygote (e.g., human AB blood type).
  • Sex-linked inheritance: genes located on sex chromosomes, often X-linked traits such as colour blindness and hemophilia.

不完全显性：杂合体表型介于两者之间（红花 × 白花 → 粉花）；共显性：两个等位基因在杂合体中都完全表现 （如人类 AB 血型）；伴性遗传：基因位于性染色体上（如 X 连锁的色盲与血友病）。

Topic 1.3 1.3 主题

How can natural and artificial selection influence changes in populations? 自然选择和人工选择如何改变种群？

Mutation creates variation; selection filters it; environments & humans decide which traits persist.

Suggested videos 推荐视频
  • Natural Selection (Crash Course Biology #14) core principles
  • Mechanisms that Increase Genetic Variation (Bozeman Science) variation sources → selection can act
  • Population Genetics: When Darwin Met Mendel (Crash Course Biology #18) allele frequencies & populations
  • Artificial Selection (Selective Breeding) (Bozeman Science) humans “choose” traits

用“逻辑链”看：先弄清楚“变异从哪里来”（variation），再看“自然选择怎么筛选”（selection），最后到“种群层面如何变化”（population genetics）。

Key Ideas 关键概念
  • Random DNA mutations introduce new alleles into a population’s gene pool.
  • Natural selection favours individuals whose traits give a selective advantage in their environment.
  • Over many generations, selection can lead to adaptation and even speciation (new species).
  • Environmental changes and human activity (artificial selection, monoculture) strongly shape evolution.

关键概念：随机突变为基因库提供新等位基因；自然选择偏爱在特定环境中“更适应”的个体， 使这些等位基因频率上升，从而形成适应甚至新物种；环境变化、人类的人工选择与单一作物 （单一栽培）都会改变进化方向。

Key Terms 重要术语
mutation mutagen carcinogen selective advantage natural selection adaptation adaptive radiation extinction artificial selection monoculture

重要术语：突变、诱变剂、致癌物、选择优势、自然选择、适应、 自适应辐射、灭绝、人工选择、单一栽培等。

Natural Selection in Action

When environments change (drought, new predators, disease, climate shifts), individuals with advantageous traits are more likely to survive and reproduce. Over time, allele frequencies shift, leading to observable changes such as different beak sizes in finches or antibiotic resistance in bacteria.

环境改变（干旱、天敌、疾病、气候变化）时，具有“有利性状”的个体生存和繁殖机会更大， 等位基因频率随之改变，长期累积就会看到种群在形态或行为上的演化，例如雀类喙的大小变化、 细菌的抗生素耐药性等。

Artificial Selection and Agriculture

Humans deliberately select traits in crops and livestock—larger fruits, higher milk yield, specific coat colours. While this can improve food production, extreme monoculture and reduced genetic diversity can increase vulnerability to disease and climate stress.

人类通过人工选择定向保留某些性状（果实更大、产奶更多、毛色特定等）。 这提高了产量，但过度单一栽培、基因多样性下降会使作物更容易在病害或气候压力下崩溃。

How and why are the genes of organisms manipulated? 人类如何以及为什么要操纵基因？

From gene cloning and GMOs to gene therapy and stem cells: powerful tools, real benefits, real risks.

  • Molecular Biology (Bozeman Science) restriction enzymes, plasmids, PCR (overview)
  • AP Biology Lab 6: Molecular Biology (Bozeman Science) transformation + gel electrophoresis

Key Ideas 关键概念
  • Biotechnology uses living systems and DNA technology to produce useful products.
  • Gene cloning and recombinant DNA allow genes to be copied and expressed in new organisms.
  • Transgenic plants and animals can provide medicine, improved crops, or research models.
  • Reproductive technologies (AI, IVF) and gene therapy offer options for treating infertility and genetic disease.
  • All biotechnology raises environmental, social, and ethical questions that must be carefully evaluated.

关键概念：生物技术利用活体系统和 DNA 技术来生产有用产物；基因克隆与重组 DNA 让基因可以在其他生物体内复制与表达；转基因植物和动物可用于药物、增产或科研模型； 人工授精、体外受精、基因治疗等技术为不孕症和遗传病提供新方案； 同时，生物技术也带来环境、社会和伦理方面的争议与风险。

Key Terms 重要术语
biotechnology cloning gene cloning recombinant DNA artificial insemination in vitro fertilization (IVF) gene therapy

重要术语：生物技术、克隆、基因克隆、重组 DNA、人工授精、 体外受精、基因治疗等。

Gene Cloning & Transgenic Organisms

In gene cloning, a gene of interest is inserted into a vector (often a bacterial plasmid) to make recombinant DNA. Transformed cells copy the plasmid and express the gene, producing proteins such as human insulin or spider silk in bacteria, plants, or animals.

基因克隆通常把目标基因插入载体（如细菌质粒）形成重组 DNA，再让细胞摄取并复制。 这样，细胞就能大量生产目标蛋白，例如人胰岛素或“蜘蛛丝蛋白”等。

Medical Uses: Reproductive Technologies & Gene Therapy

  • Artificial insemination: sperm is collected, concentrated, and introduced directly into the uterus.
  • In vitro fertilization (IVF): eggs and sperm meet in a lab dish; early embryos are transferred back to the uterus.
  • Gene therapy: a “healthy” version of a gene is delivered to cells to compensate for a faulty version.

人工授精：收集、浓缩精子后直接注入子宫；体外受精：在实验室中让卵子与精子结合，再将早期胚胎移回子宫； 基因治疗：把“健康版本”的基因送入细胞，用来替代或补偿缺陷基因。

Risks, Ethics, and Society

Concerns include possible effects on ecosystems (superweeds, superbugs), long-term health impacts, unequal access to treatments, and questions about who owns and controls genetic information. Ethical analysis is a core part of learning about biotechnology.

风险包括：转基因作物与野生种杂交产生“超级杂草/超级害虫”、长期食用安全性未知、 治疗资源分配不均，以及“谁有权使用和控制基因信息”等问题。 因此，伦理与社会讨论是学习生物技术不可缺少的一部分。
