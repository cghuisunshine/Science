Inquiry Question
探究问题
Given that you can’t get something from nothing, how do
chemical reactions work?
既然你不可能从“无”中得到“有”，
那么化学反应究竟是怎样发生的？

We know that products can’t just magically appear but are produced from reactants in chemical reactions. It is also known that chemical reactions must obey the Law of Conservation of Mass which states that atoms are neither created nor destroyed. The mass of the reactants has to equal the mass of the products for all chemical reactions. 我们知道，产物不会凭空神奇地出现，而是由反应物在化学反应中生成。我们也知道，化学反应必须遵守质量守恒定律，即原子既不会被创造，也不会被消灭。对于所有化学反应，反应物的质量必须等于产物的质量。

In the production of water from hydrogen and oxygen, the reactants are H2 and O2 while the product is H2O. This reaction shows there are four atoms of hydrogen both before and afterwards and two atoms of oxygen on both sides of the reaction. The number of atoms of each element is balanced. Mass would be conserved. 在由氢和氧生成水的过程中，反应物是 H2 和 O2，产物是 H2O。这个反应表明，反应前后都有四个氢原子，反应式两边也都有两个氧原子。每种元素的原子数是平衡的，因此质量得到守恒。

The coefficients in front of the chemical formulas are changed, as needed, to ensure the number of each atom on each side of the equation is "balanced". 为了确保方程式两边每种原子的数目“平衡”，需要按需要调整化学式前面的系数。

If there is an excess of one of the reactants, more than what is needed to make the product, then it will be left over after the reaction. 如果某一种反应物过量，也就是多于生成产物所需的量，那么反应结束后它会剩余。

For this project, you will be running a simulation to illustrate how reactions obey the Law of Conservation of Mass. The simulation shows how each atom must be balanced in a reaction. 在这个项目中，你将运行一个模拟来说明反应如何遵守质量守恒定律。这个模拟展示了在一个反应中，每一种原子都必须保持平衡。

General Instructions
一般说明
The goal of this project is to gain a better understanding of conservation of mass by working through the simulation below.
本项目的目标是通过完成下面的模拟，更好地理解质量守恒。

Materials you’ll need: 你需要的材料：

  • PhET simulation PhET 模拟
  • Snipping tool or alternate method of doing screenshots 截图工具或其他截图方法
  • Presentation software 演示文稿软件

Ideas and Hints: 思路与提示：

  1. If you have a Windows based PC, search for the Snipping tool app on your computer and open it. If you have an Apple computer you can take screenshots by pressing 4 while holding the command and shift buttons. 如果你使用 Windows 电脑，请在计算机上搜索并打开 Snipping Tool 应用。如果你使用 Apple 电脑，可以同时按住 Command 和 Shift 键，再按 4 来截图。
  2. Open the PhET simulation. 打开 PhET 模拟。
  3. Work through the simulation by building the different sandwiches, making the different molecular products and completing the games. 通过完成模拟中的不同三明治搭建、不同分子产物的生成以及各个游戏关卡来进行练习。
  4. You will need 10 screenshots: 你需要 10 张截图：
    • The images must show successful building of the 3 different sandwich types, show the 3 different product molecules and show any 4 game examples (including at least one from each level of the game). 这些图片必须展示 3 种不同三明治类型的成功搭建、3 种不同产物分子，以及任意 4 个游戏示例（其中至少每个游戏级别各有 1 个）。
  5. These screenshots will need to be combined in presentation software. It could then be saved as a .pdf document for submission. 这些截图需要在演示文稿软件中整合，然后可以保存为 .pdf 文件提交。

Project submission: 项目提交：

Submit your document into the Chemistry project drop box. 将你的文档提交到化学项目投递箱。
