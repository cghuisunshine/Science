  6. Fe2O3 (s) + 3 H2 (g) → 2 Fe (s) + 3 H2O (l) (single replacement)
  7. CaCO3 (s) → CaO (s) + CO2 (g) (other or decomposition)

  10. 2 NaCl (l) → 2 Na (l) + Cl2 (g) (decomposition)
  11. 2 Al (s) + 3 H2SO4 (aq) → 3 H2 (g) + Al2(SO4)3 (aq) (single replacement)
  12. H3PO4 (aq) + 3 NH4OH (aq) → 3 H2O (l) + (NH4)3PO4 (aq) (double replacement)

  14. 4 Al (s) + 3 O2 (g) → 2 Al2O3 (s) (formation)
