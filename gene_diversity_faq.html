A Case Study in Genetic Diversity – The Cheetah
基因多样性的案例研究——猎豹

We’ll use cheetahs as an example to help you understand the importance of genetic diversity in a population. The cheetah has been called the “poster child” for genetic diversity.

我们将以猎豹为例，帮助你理解“种群中的基因多样性”为什么重要。猎豹常被称为基因多样性问题的“典型案例”。

20 000 years ago, cheetahs lived in Africa, Asia, Europe and North America.

两万年前，猎豹分布在非洲、亚洲、欧洲和北美洲。

About 10 000 years ago, all but one species of cheetah became extinct. This population decline forced close relatives to breed. As a result, the cheetah became genetically inbred. Inbred animals suffer from a lack of genetic diversity. As cheetah populations continue to decline, there is less and less genetic diversity in the species.

约一万年前，除一种外的猎豹都灭绝了。种群数量骤降使近亲被迫繁殖，导致猎豹发生近交。近交的动物会面临基因多样性不足的问题。随着猎豹种群继续下降，物种内的基因多样性也越来越低。

Genes store inherited genetic information. Even though they may determine the same trait, each gene is different in every individual. By studying genetic diversity, scientists can figure out how animals in a population are related. This helps them predict how changes may affect the population.

基因储存着遗传信息。即使决定同一种性状，不同个体的基因也可能存在差异。研究基因多样性可以帮助科学家判断种群中个体之间的亲缘关系，并预测环境变化可能对种群造成的影响。

Cheetahs have much lower genetic diversity than other cats. This can cause lowered resistance to disease. Viruses that might affect one cheetah could be deadly to every cheetah. Other species are more diverse, so some individuals will naturally be more resistant to a virus. Since all cheetahs have a low genetic diversity, the virus could affect them all equally.

与其他猫科动物相比，猎豹的基因多样性更低，这会降低它们对疾病的抵抗力。某种病毒可能对一只猎豹致病，也可能对所有猎豹都致命。其他物种更具多样性，通常会有部分个体天生对病毒更有抵抗力；但由于猎豹整体多样性低，病毒可能对它们造成更一致、更严重的影响。

Questions（问题）

1. Why is genetic diversity important?
为什么基因多样性很重要？

Genetic diversity is important because it helps populations survive diseases and environmental changes. When individuals have different genes, some will naturally be more resistant, increasing the chance the species will survive.
基因多样性之所以重要，是因为它能帮助种群更好地应对疾病和环境变化。不同个体拥有不同基因时，通常会有一些个体更耐受或更有抵抗力，从而提高整个物种存活的机会。

2. What most likely caused the cheetahs to lose genetic diversity?
猎豹最可能因为什么而失去基因多样性？

A major population decline or near-extinction event (population bottleneck) forced close relatives to breed, causing inbreeding and loss of genetic variation.
由于种群数量大幅下降或接近灭绝（种群瓶颈），近亲被迫繁殖，导致近交并减少遗传变异。

3. What are some examples of genetic diversity in humans?
人类有哪些基因多样性的例子？

Examples include eye color, hair color and texture, skin tone, height, blood type, and facial features. These differences are caused by genetic variation.
例如：眼睛颜色、头发颜色与发质、肤色、身高、血型、面部特征等。这些差异来自遗传变异。
