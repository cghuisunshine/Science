2.6 Acids, Bases and Salts2.6 酸、碱和盐

A reading page for the key ideas, naming rules, ion behaviour, and common reactions involving acids, bases, and salts.本阅读页整理了酸、碱和盐的重要概念、命名规则、离子行为，以及常见化学反应。

This section explains how acids and bases are identified, how pH is used to compare them, how indicators work, and how salts form in neutralization and other reactions.本节说明如何辨认酸和碱、如何用 pH 比较它们、指示剂如何发挥作用，以及盐如何在中和反应和其他反应中形成。

General Information基本信息

Acids and bases are very common.酸和碱非常常见。

  • Many familiar compounds are acids or bases.许多我们熟悉的化合物都是酸或碱。
  • Classification as acids or bases is based on chemical composition.一种物质被归类为酸或碱，是依据它的化学组成。

Acids and bases can be very dangerous.酸和碱可能非常危险。

  • Both can be very corrosive.两者都可能具有很强的腐蚀性。
  • NEVER try to identify an acid or base by taste or touch.绝对不要用味觉或触摸来辨认酸或碱。

The strength of acids and bases in measured on the pH scale.酸和碱的强弱是用 pH 标度来测量的。

pH below 7 = acidicpH 小于 7 = 酸性
pH 7 = neutralpH 等于 7 = 中性
pH above 7 = basicpH 大于 7 = 碱性
  • Each decrease of 1 on the pH scale indicates 10 times (10 x) more acidic.pH 每降低 1，表示酸性增强 10 倍（10 x）。
  • For example, pH 4 is ten times more acidic than pH 5.例如，pH 4 的酸性是 pH 5 的 10 倍。
  • pH 3 is 1 000 times (1 000 x) more acidic than pH 6.pH 3 的酸性是 pH 6 的 1 000 倍（1 000 x）。

pH IndicatorspH 指示剂

The pH of acids and bases cannot be determined by sight.酸和碱的 pH 不能靠肉眼直接判断。

  • Instead, pH is measured by other chemicals, called indicators, or by a pH meter that measures the electrical conductivity of the solution.相反，pH 要靠其他化学物质来测量，这些物质叫做指示剂；也可以用 pH 计，通过测量溶液的导电性来判断。

pH indicators change colour based on the pH of the solution they are placed in.pH 指示剂会根据所放入溶液的 pH 改变颜色。

  • Litmus is the most common indicator, used on litmus paper.石蕊是最常见的指示剂，通常用在石蕊试纸上。
  • Two colours of litmus paper: Blue = basic and Red = acidic.石蕊试纸有两种颜色：蓝色表示碱性，红色表示酸性。
  • Blue = pH above 7, Red = pH below 7.蓝色 = pH 大于 7，红色 = pH 小于 7。
  • A universal indicator contains many indicators that turn different colours at different pH values and can be in liquid form or on paper strips.通用指示剂由多种在不同 pH 下会变不同颜色的指示剂组成，可以是液体，也可以涂在试纸条上。
  • A pH meter uses electrical probes to measure how solutions conduct electricity.pH 计使用电极来测量溶液导电的情况。
  • Indicators change colour at different pH values, so different indicators are used to identify different pH values.不同指示剂会在不同 pH 范围内变色，所以要用不同指示剂来判断不同的 pH。
  • Bromothymol blue for pH 6 - 7.6, phenolphthalein for pH 8.2 - 10.溴麝香草酚蓝适用于 pH 6 - 7.6，酚酞适用于 pH 8.2 - 10。
  • Many natural sources, such as beets and cabbage, can also act as indicators.许多天然物质，如甜菜和卷心菜，也可以充当指示剂。

Acids酸

If you know a compound's chemical formula, you may be able to identify it as an acid.如果你知道某种化合物的化学式，你可能就能判断它是不是酸。

  • Acids often behave like acids only when dissolved in water.酸通常只有在溶于水时才表现出酸性。
  • Therefore, acids are often written with the subscript (aq) = aquatic = in water.因此，酸常常写成带有下标 (aq)，表示 aqueous，也就是在水中。

The chemical formula of an acid usually starts with hydrogen (H).酸的化学式通常以氢（H）开头。

  • Acids with a carbon usually have the C written first.含有碳的酸通常把 C 写在最前面。
  • HCl(aq) hydrochloric acidHCl(aq) 盐酸
  • HNO3(aq) nitric acidHNO3(aq) 硝酸
  • CH3COOH(aq) acetic acidCH3COOH(aq) 乙酸

Hydrogen + ...-ide = Hydro...ic acidHydrogen + ...-ide = Hydro...ic acid（命名规律）

For acids formed from hydrogen plus a polyatomic ion ending in -ate对于由氢和以 -ate 结尾的多原子离子组成的酸

Hydrogen + ...-ate = ...ic acidHydrogen + ...-ate = ...ic acid（命名规律）

H2CO3(aq) = hydrogen carbonate = carbonic acidH2CO3(aq) = hydrogen carbonate = carbonic acid（碳酸）

Hydrogen + ...-ite = ...ous acidHydrogen + ...-ite = ...ous acid（命名规律）

Bases碱

If you know a compound's chemical formula, you may be able to identify it as a base.如果你知道某种化合物的化学式，你可能就能判断它是不是碱。

  • Bases, like acids, often behave like bases only when dissolved in water.碱和酸一样，通常只有在溶于水时才表现出碱性。
  • Therefore, bases are often written with the subscript (aq) = aquatic = in water.因此，碱也常写成带有下标 (aq)，表示在水中。

The chemical formula of a base usually ends with hydroxide (OH).碱的化学式通常以 hydroxide（OH）结尾。

Examples of common bases常见碱的例子

  • NaOH(aq)
  • Mg(OH)2(aq)
  • Ca(OH)2(aq)
  • NH4OH(aq)

Naming Bases碱的命名

To name a base you simply name the cation (metal) and then add hydroxide.给碱命名时，只要先写出阳离子（金属）名称，再加上 hydroxide。

  • NaOH(aq) - Sodium hydroxideNaOH(aq) - 氢氧化钠
  • Mg(OH)2(aq) - Magnesium hydroxideMg(OH)2(aq) - 氢氧化镁

Production of Ions离子的产生

Acids and bases can conduct electricity because they release ions in solution.酸和碱能导电，因为它们在溶液中会释放离子。

  • Acids release hydrogen ions, H+(aq).酸会释放氢离子，H+(aq)。
  • Bases release hydroxide ions, OH-(aq).碱会释放氢氧根离子，OH-(aq)。

The pH of a solution refers to the concentration of ions it has.溶液的 pH 与其中离子的浓度有关。

Square brackets are used to signify concentration: [H⁺(aq)], [OH⁻(aq)]方括号表示浓度：[H⁺(aq)]、[OH⁻(aq)]。

High [H⁺(aq)] = low pH, very acidic高 [H⁺(aq)] = 低 pH，酸性很强。

High [OH⁻(aq)] = high pH, very basic高 [OH⁻(aq)] = 高 pH，碱性很强。

A solution cannot have BOTH high [H⁺(aq)] and [OH⁻(aq)]; they cancel each other out and form water. This process is called neutralization.一种溶液不可能同时具有很高的 [H⁺(aq)] 和 [OH⁻(aq)]；它们会彼此中和并形成水。这个过程叫做中和。

[H⁺(aq)] + [OH⁻(aq)] → H₂O(l)[H⁺(aq)] + [OH⁻(aq)] → H₂O(l)

Salts盐

Salts are ionic compounds formed when acids and bases react.盐是酸和碱反应时形成的离子化合物。

  • Salts are also produced when oxides or carbonates react with acids, or when metals react with acids.当氧化物或碳酸盐与酸反应，或金属与酸反应时，也会生成盐。

Table salt, NaCl, is found in seawater, salt lakes, or rock deposits.食盐 NaCl 存在于海水、盐湖或岩盐矿床中。

  • Salt was once very valuable as a commodity.盐曾经是一种非常有价值的商品。
  • Iodine is now added to salt to minimize goiter (a disease of the thyroid).现在人们会在盐中加入碘，以减少甲状腺肿（甲状腺疾病）的发生。

NaCl is only one kind of salt.NaCl 只是盐的一种。

  • A salt is made up of a positive ion from a base and negative ion from an acid.盐由来自碱的阳离子和来自酸的阴离子组成。
  • Salts are found in many things.许多东西中都含有盐。
  • In batteries, explosives, and fertilizers.例如电池、炸药和肥料中。
  • In multivitamins.例如复合维生素中。
  • In many living cells.例如许多生物细胞中。

Neutralization中和反应

Neutralization reactions occur when an acid and a base react to produce a salt and water.中和反应发生在酸和碱反应并生成盐和水的时候。

Metal and Non-metal Oxides金属氧化物和非金属氧化物

Metal oxides react with water to form bases.金属氧化物与水反应会形成碱。

Na₂O(s) + H₂O(l) → 2 NaOH(aq)

Non-metal oxides react with water to form acids.非金属氧化物与水反应会形成酸。

SO₂(g) + H₂O(l) → H₂SO₃(aq)

Non-metal oxides are formed from the burning of fossil fuels.非金属氧化物会在化石燃料燃烧时产生。

Add water in the atmosphere = acid precipitation.再与大气中的水结合，就会形成酸性降水。

Acid Reactions with Metals and Carbonates酸与金属和碳酸盐的反应

Acids and Metals酸与金属

  • The most reactive metals, at the bottom of groups 1 and 2, react vigorously with water and acids.第 1 族和第 2 族底部最活泼的金属，会与水和酸发生剧烈反应。
  • All other metals are less reactive than those in groups 1 and 2.其他金属的活泼性都低于第 1 族和第 2 族中的这些金属。
  • When metals do react with acids, H₂ gas is usually released.金属与酸反应时，通常会放出 H₂ 气体。

2 HCl(aq) + Mg(s) → MgCl₂(s) + H₂(g)

Acids and Carbonates酸与碳酸盐

  • Carbonates (CO₃²⁻) neutralize acids, protecting locations with natural carbonate supplies from acid precipitation.碳酸盐（CO₃²⁻）能中和酸，因此天然富含碳酸盐的地区更能抵御酸性降水。

H₂SO₄(aq) + CaCO₃(s) → CaSO₄(s) + H₂O(aq) + CO₂(g)

sulphuric acid + calcium carbonate ➝ calcium sulfate + water + carbon dioxide硫酸 + 碳酸钙 ➝ 硫酸钙 + 水 + 二氧化碳
