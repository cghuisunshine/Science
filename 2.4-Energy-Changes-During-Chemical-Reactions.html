2.4 Energy Changes During Chemical Reactions2.4 化学反应中的能量变化

Kinetic and Potential Energy Definitions动能与势能的定义

Kinetic Energy动能

Kinetic energy is the energy due to movement.动能是由于运动而具有的能量。

"Kinetic" means "moving." Hence, kinetic energy is the energy due to movement. A thrown ball, a rolling log, a flying atom, and the electrons orbiting the nucleus of an atom all have kinetic energy.“Kinetic” 的意思是“运动的”。因此，动能就是由运动产生的能量。被抛出的球、滚动的木头、飞行中的原子，以及围绕原子核运动的电子都具有动能。

Potential Energy势能

Potential energy is stored energy, energy which can be converted into kinetic energy when the conditions are right.势能是储存起来的能量，在条件合适时可以转化为动能。

A rock poised at the top of a hill has potential energy. Give it a push and it rolls downhill converting its potential energy to kinetic. The potential energy in the gasoline burned in an automobile engine is transformed partly into the motion of the car. Because the internal combustion engine is notoriously inefficient, much of the potential energy of the gasoline is converted into wasted heat energy, which is a form of kinetic energy.一块位于山顶边缘的石头具有势能。只要轻轻一推，它就会向下滚动，把势能转化为动能。汽车发动机中燃烧的汽油所含的势能，有一部分会转化为汽车的运动。由于内燃机效率并不高，汽油的大量势能会转化成浪费掉的热能，而热能也是动能的一种形式。

Bond Breaking and Bond Forming断键与成键

For a reaction to occur, reactant particles must collide, creating an opportunity for old bonds to break and new bonds to form. Any one collision may be successful or unsuccessful depending on two factors. These factors are the geometry of the collision (head on versus side swipes, for example) and the energy of the collision (faster moving molecules with more kinetic energy are more likely to react).要发生化学反应，反应物粒子必须彼此碰撞，这样旧键才有机会断裂，新键才有机会形成。一次碰撞是否成功，取决于两个因素：碰撞的几何方式（例如正面碰撞还是擦边而过）以及碰撞的能量（运动更快、动能更大的分子更容易发生反应）。

Each chemical reaction has an activation energy or minimum energy required for the reaction to occur. If the colliding atoms or molecules generate sufficient activation energy (kinetic energy is converted to potential energy in the collision) then bonds in reactant molecules will break and atoms can re-arrange and form new bonds in the products that are formed. If collisions occur that do not generate this activation energy, the reactant atoms and molecules will repel each other with no reaction taking place.每一种化学反应都有活化能，也就是反应发生所需的最小能量。如果碰撞中的原子或分子产生了足够的活化能（在碰撞中，动能会转化为势能），那么反应物分子中的键就会断裂，原子便能重新排列，并在生成物中形成新的键。如果碰撞没有产生足够的活化能，那么反应物原子和分子就会彼此排斥，不会发生反应。

The bonds within a chemical compound are a source of potential energy. The process of breaking bonds requires energy and then energy is released as new bonds form. If more energy is absorbed than released, the reaction is endothermic (container will feel cold to the touch). If less energy is absorbed than released, the reaction is exothermic (container radiates heat that you can sense on the back of your hand). An analogy for a bond is a partly stretched elastic band where PE is present. Energy must be applied to break the bond, but once broken, a different amount of energy is released.化合物中的化学键是势能的来源。断键过程需要吸收能量，而新键形成时会释放能量。如果吸收的能量多于释放的能量，这个反应就是吸热反应（容器摸起来会发冷）。如果吸收的能量少于释放的能量，这个反应就是放热反应（容器会向外散热，你可以在手背上感觉到热）。可以把化学键类比成一条被部分拉伸的橡皮筋，其中储存着势能。要把它弄断，必须输入能量；但一旦断开，又会释放出另一数量的能量。

The changes that occur in a chemical reaction typically involve breaking of the bonds that exist in the reactant molecules. This process requires energy. New bonds are formed as new chemical substances are produced, the products in the reaction. This process releases energy.化学反应中的变化通常涉及反应物分子中已有化学键的断裂，这个过程需要能量。当新的化学物质，也就是生成物，形成时，会产生新的化学键，而这个过程会释放能量。

Exothermic Reactions放热反应

These are reactions which release energy. Energy in chemical reactions is measured in kilojoules (kJ). Consider the following hypothetical reaction:这类反应会释放能量。化学反应中的能量通常以千焦（kJ）为单位来测量。请看下面这个假设反应：

x2 + y2 → 2 xy

50 kJ of energy are required to break the bonds that hold the x's and y's together in their respective molecules, but 100 kJ is released when xy forms.要断开 x 和 y 在各自分子中所形成的键，需要 50 kJ 的能量；但当 xy 形成时，会释放出 100 kJ 的能量。

50 kJ + x2 + y2 → 2 xy + 100 kJ

Simplified:简化后：

x2 + y2 → 2 xy + 50 kJ

The net result is that 50 kJ of energy are released.净结果是释放了 50 kJ 的能量。

Reactions of this sort which produce energy are called exothermic reactions. "Exo" means "out" and "therm" refers to heat. So, "exothermic" means that heat is coming out, or being produced.这种会产生能量的反应叫做放热反应。“Exo” 的意思是“向外”，“therm” 指的是“热”。因此，“exothermic” 的意思就是热向外释放出来，或者说有热产生。

Reactants → Products + energy反应物 → 生成物 + 能量

An example of an exothermic reaction occurs during cellular respiration in animals and plants. The reaction involves "burning" sugar (glucose C6H12O6(s)) to produce energy. Oxygen from the air is required.动物和植物进行细胞呼吸时就会发生一个放热反应。这个反应涉及“燃烧”糖类，也就是葡萄糖 C6H12O6(s)，以产生能量。这个过程需要空气中的氧气。

C6H12O6(s) + 6 O2(g) → 6 CO2(g) + 6 H2O(l) + 2 803 kJ

Endothermic Reactions吸热反应

There is a net requirement or absorption of energy in some chemical reactions. The bond breaking process in the reactants requires more energy than is released in total. Energy from an external source must be absorbed.有些化学反应总体上需要吸收能量。反应物中的断键过程所需的能量，大于整个过程中所释放的能量，因此必须从外界吸收能量。

Energy + Reactants → Products能量 + 反应物 → 生成物

Such reactions which absorb energy are called endothermic reactions. "Endo" means "into".这种吸收能量的反应叫做吸热反应。“Endo” 的意思是“向内”。

Plants have the ability to use the sun's energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. This is the opposite process of cellular respiration and is called photosynthesis:植物能够利用太阳能，把二氧化碳和水转化成葡萄糖和氧气。这个过程与细胞呼吸相反，叫做光合作用：

6 CO2(g) + 6 H2O(l) + 2 803 kJ (from the sun) → C6H12O6(s) + 6 O2(g)
