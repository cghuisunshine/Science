Science 10 Study Tips 学习提示

1. The atomic number tells how many protons are in an atom. 原子序数表示原子中有多少个质子。

2. Elements in the same group often have similar chemical properties. 同一族的元素通常有相似的化学性质。

3. Metals are mostly on the left, non-metals are mostly on the right, and metalloids are along the staircase. 金属大多在左边，非金属大多在右边，类金属通常沿着“楼梯线”分布。

4. For Science 10 naming compounds, pay special attention to common ions such as Group 1 = +1, Group 2 = +2, halogens = -1, oxygen family often = -2. 对于 Science 10 的化合物命名，要特别注意常见离子电荷，如第1族通常是 +1，第2族通常是 +2，卤素通常是 -1，氧族通常常见为 -2。

5. Click an element to review useful facts before quizzes. 点击元素可复习测验前常用知识点。
