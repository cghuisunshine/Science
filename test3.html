中文

English

Fruit flies are often seen around rotting fruit. They have a short life cycle, reproduce quickly, and have relatively few chromosomes that are easy to observe, so they are commonly used in genetics experiments.

The figure shows the chromosomes in somatic cells and gametes of male and female fruit flies.
